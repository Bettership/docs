You're learning for a reason. You might want to lead a difficult conversation well, make stronger product decisions, or understand a subject that keeps drawing you back.

Bettership is a personal learning workspace with a team of AI agents. It keeps the material you bring connected to what you're trying to get better at, with help to understand it, practice it, and make something useful from it.

## Start with something that matters

Bring an article, podcast, book, video, course material, capture, or your own notes. Give it a purpose: what would you like to understand or do better because of it?

You can name that direction as a **Focus Area**. It can be broad, like thinking more strategically, or specific, like asking better customer interview questions. You don't need to design a curriculum before you begin.

## Four kinds of help

- **Steward** helps you find your way around Bettership and keep your personal context and Focus Areas accurate.
- **Scout** helps you find, assess, and organize learning resources.
- **Coach** helps you understand ideas, practice, and learn from an attempt.
- **Maker** turns ideas from your Library into prompts, context packs, and skills for your own AI tools.

The agents share workspace context. You choose what to bring in, what to work on, and which suggestions to use.

## Who it's for

Bettership is for self-directed professionals already investing in getting better. You may have good material and a real reason to learn, but want help making that effort useful without building a learning system of your own.

You can begin with a question, a resource, a practice need, or something you want to make. There's no required route through all four agents.
