Ask Maker to turn what you're learning into something you use in the AI tools you work in.

## What to ask

For example:

- “Use these interview guides to make a prompt that helps me spot leading questions. I'll use it in ChatGPT.”
- “Make a skill that scores my product specs against what my product resources teach.”
- “Make me a startup advisor from my reading, and use my plan review skill in it.”
- “Update my interview prep skill with what's new in my Library.”

Say what the result should help you do, where you'll use it, and which resources to draw from.

## Where to find Maker

Open **Apply → Maker** for a conversation. The **Studio** holds Maker's drafts alongside the items you've already made.

## What Maker does

**Makes four kinds of item.** A **prompt** gives an AI tool reusable instructions for one job. A **context pack** gives it background about you and how you work. A **skill** packages a method, with references, such as a way to critique interview questions. A **subagent** is a specialist: a role and the skills it uses, packaged together. Subagents are part of Plus and Max.

**Proposes before a bigger item.** For a skill or a subagent, Maker says what it would make, from which resources, and what it's assuming, then waits for your go-ahead. A direct ask for a prompt can go straight to making it.

**Shows the result.** Each item has saved files, the resources it drew on, and how to use it. A prompt, skill, or subagent also comes with a demo run on your own material. Maker reads it back and names its weakest part.

**Reuses what you made.** Name a skill you've already made, and Maker copies it into a subagent unchanged. A later change to that skill reaches the subagent the next time you ask Maker to revise it.

**Revises on request.** Say what should change, and Maker makes a new version. It changes only the files your request needs and keeps the rest as they were. Earlier versions stay.

**Makes items for your AI tools.** Once an AI tool is connected to Bettership, as [Use what you made in your AI tools](/apply/use-what-you-made-in-your-ai-tools) shows, ask for an item that works through that connection. It reaches your AI tools as soon as it's made, along with its later versions. A subagent's page in the Studio says where it works: some tools run it as a separate subagent, some take on its role for a conversation, and some take a version of the role you paste into their instructions.
