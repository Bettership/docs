Maker can make four kinds of item for the AI tools you work in, and each fits a different job. Knowing them helps you ask for the one you need. To get one made, ask Maker in **Apply → Maker**, or start it from **Apply → Studio**, where your items are kept.

Say you've finished a course on writing product specs, and next week you're drafting one in Claude. On its own, Claude knows nothing about the course. You'd paste in your notes, explain the framework again, and hope it sticks. With something Maker made from that course, Claude already has it. As you learn more, Maker can revise what it made, and when it's connected to your AI tools, they get the new version.

## A prompt does one job

A prompt is a set of instructions for a task you do again and again, like getting ready for a customer interview. You paste it into the tool, or ask for it by name once the tool is connected to Bettership, and the tool does the job the way your resources taught it. [Use what you made in your AI tools](/apply/use-what-you-made-in-your-ai-tools) covers connecting.

## A context pack is background about you

A context pack tells an AI tool who you are, what you know about a topic, and how you work, drawn from your Library, everything you've added to Bettership. With it in place, you stop explaining yourself every time you start a new chat, and answers fit your situation.

## A skill is a method you run on your own work

A skill takes a method an expert taught and turns it into something you run on your own work. For example, a skill might score your product spec against the principles you've been learning, name its weakest parts, and suggest a rewrite. A prompt handles one moment. A skill applies a whole method, with the scoring guide or checklist it needs.

## A subagent is a specialist

A subagent brings together a role (who it acts as, and when it's the right one to call on), the skills it uses, and only the background it needs. For example, a startup advisor built from your reading might review a plan with one skill and turn a growth idea into a first step with another. You ask it for help, and it works out which of its skills fits. Subagents are part of the Plus and Max plans.
