Making something useful shouldn't mean it only works inside Bettership. Maker creates prompts, context packs, skills, and subagents you can carry into your own AI tools.

## Made items have files

A reusable prompt carries instructions you can use again. A context pack gives another AI tool relevant background. A skill packages an approach you want that tool to follow.

Each item has its own page in **Apply → Studio**. The page shows the resources it drew on, keeps its revised versions, and has its files to download. The files and use instructions help you take the work elsewhere for personal use.

Downloading files doesn't install them in another tool. You still place or attach them there and follow the instructions for that tool. Maker can explain the steps, but there is no automatic sync or scheduled refresh.

## A subagent is a specialist

A skill is one method. A subagent is a specialist built from your learning that knows which of its skills to use for what: a role, the skills it uses, and only the background it needs, packaged together. A startup advisor might pressure-test a plan with one skill and turn a growth idea into a first step with another.

AI tools take a subagent in different ways. Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex run it as a separate subagent from a file saved in their agents folder. Claude chat and ChatGPT take on its role for one conversation. An always-on agent, such as Grok Bot, takes a version of the role you paste into its instructions. A subagent's page in the Studio says where it works, and its files include one for each of these, which Bettership writes from its role.

## Personal use has a boundary

“Yours to keep for personal use” doesn't give you ownership of someone else's book, course, or method. Keep the rights attached to the original material in mind when using or sharing an output.

## A made item isn't an account export

Downloading Maker's files is different from taking a copy of all the personal data in your Bettership account. A self-service account export isn't available yet. You can request a copy by emailing [privacy@bettership.ai](mailto:privacy@bettership.ai).
