When you're done, Claude can read your Library and use what Maker makes for them.

## Before you start

- A Plus or Max plan. Beta access counts.
- The Claude desktop app.

## Connect Claude

1. In the desktop app, open **Settings → Connectors & MCP** and copy the address it shows: `https://api.bettership.ai/mcp`.
2. In the Claude desktop app, choose **Customize** in Claude's sidebar, then the **Connectors** tab.
3. Choose **Add** at the top right, then **Add custom connector**.
4. Enter Bettership in **Name** and the address in **MCP server URL**, then choose **Continue**.
5. **Sign in now** and **Use Claude's published identity** are already selected. Leave them and choose **Add**.
6. Claude's Bettership connector page says "You're not connected to Bettership yet." Choose **Connect**. Your browser opens.
7. If your browser shows a page asking "Finish connecting a connector?", choose **Continue connecting**.
8. A Bettership page in your browser lists what Claude is asking to read. Choose **Allow Access**.
9. Sign in with **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or **Email me a code**.
10. On "Use this Bettership account?", check the email address and choose **Continue**. Your browser shows "Connected".

## Make an item for your AI tools

1. In the desktop app, open **Apply → Studio** and choose **Make new**. The "Make something new" window opens.
2. Under "How should it work?", choose **Through my Bettership connection**, not **A self-contained download**.
3. Under "What should it do?", say what it's for.
4. Choose **Make it**. Maker makes it in a chat, and your AI tools get it and its later versions.
5. When the item's card in the chat reads "Live", open the item from **Apply → Studio**. Beside **Copy prompt** and **Download files**, a status button says whether your AI tools can read it. Before Claude is connected, it reads "Ready for your AI tools".

Choose it to open a panel with:

- **What your AI tools can read** lists the item's files.
- **Pause automatic updates** holds later versions back until you choose **Turn on automatic updates**.
- **Unpublish** takes it out of your AI tools. The button then reads "Not in your AI tools".

## Use it in Claude

In a new chat, ask for what you need. For example:

- "What does my Bettership Library say about pricing?"
- "Use my interview prep skill from Bettership on this job description."

Claude shows "Used Bettership" when it reads from your account, and names the resources it drew on.

## Check it worked

In the desktop app, **Settings → Connectors & MCP** lists the connection under "Authorized connections" as "Connected", with the time Claude last read from it.

To stop Claude reading, choose **Disconnect** there or on the Bettership page in Claude's **Connectors** tab. Copies Claude already received stay in Claude.
