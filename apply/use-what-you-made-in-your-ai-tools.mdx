When you're done, Claude can read your Library and use what Maker makes for it, by name in any chat.

## Before you start

- A Plus or Max plan. Beta access counts.
- The Claude desktop app.

## Connect Claude

1. In the desktop app, open **Settings → Connectors & MCP**. Copy the address it shows, `https://api.bettership.ai/mcp`.
2. In the Claude desktop app, open **Customize → Connectors**.
3. Choose **Add** at the top right of the Connectors tab.
4. Choose **Add custom connector**.
5. Enter Bettership in **Name** and the address in **MCP server URL**.
6. Choose **Continue**. Claude has already selected **Sign in now** and **Use Claude's published identity**. Leave them.
7. Choose **Add**. Claude opens its Bettership page, which says "You're not connected to Bettership yet."
8. Choose **Connect**. Your browser opens. If it asks "Finish connecting a connector?", choose **Continue connecting**.
9. On the Bettership page that lists what Claude is asking to read, choose **Allow Access**.
10. Sign in with **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or **Email me a code**.
11. On "Use this Bettership account?", check the email address and choose **Continue**. Your browser shows "Connected".

## Make an item for Claude

1. In the desktop app, open **Apply → Studio**.
2. Choose **Make new** at the top right. The "Make something new" window opens.
3. Under "How should it work?", choose **Through my Bettership connection**. The window says the item and its later versions reach your AI tools "as soon as it is made".
4. Under "What should it do?", enter what it's for.
5. Choose **Make it**. Maker's chat opens and Maker starts.
6. Wait for the item's card in Maker's reply, showing its kind, its name, and "Live · 1 file". Claude can now use it.

## Use it in Claude

In a new Claude chat, ask for what you need. For example:

- "What does my Bettership Library say about pricing?"
- "Use my interview prep skill from Bettership on this job description."

Claude shows "Used Bettership" when it reads your account, and names the resources it drew on.

## Pause or remove an item

Open **Apply → Studio** and choose the item's card. After **Copy prompt** and **Download files** is a third button, whose label says whether your AI tools can read the item. Choose it, then:

- **Pause automatic updates** keeps later versions out of Claude. The same button then reads **Turn on automatic updates**.
- **Unpublish** takes the item out of your AI tools. The button then reads "Not in your AI tools".

## Check it worked

In the desktop app, **Settings → Connectors & MCP** lists the connection under "Authorized connections" as "Connected", with the time Claude last read from it. To stop Claude reading, choose **Disconnect** there. Claude keeps what it already received.
