When you're done, Claude can read your Library and use the prompts, context packs, and skills you publish from the Studio.

## Before you start

- A Plus or Max plan. Beta access counts.
- The Claude desktop app.
- A prompt, context pack, or skill you made with Maker, if you want Claude to use one.

## Connect Claude

1. In the desktop app, open **Settings → Connectors & MCP** and copy the address under "Add this address as a remote MCP server in your AI tool": `https://api.bettership.ai/mcp`.
2. In the Claude desktop app, choose **Customize** in Claude's sidebar, then the **Connectors** tab.
3. Choose **Add** at the top right, then **Add custom connector**.
4. Enter Bettership in **Name** and the address in **MCP server URL**, then choose **Continue**.
5. The next screen shows **Sign in now** and **Use Claude's published identity**, each already selected. Leave both selected and choose **Add**.
6. Claude shows its Bettership connector page: "You're not connected to Bettership yet." Choose **Connect**. Your browser opens.
7. If your browser shows a page asking "Finish connecting a connector?", choose **Continue connecting** on it.
8. A Bettership page in your browser lists what Claude is asking to read: your Library, and the items you publish. Choose **Allow Access**.
9. Sign in with **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or **Email me a code**.
10. On "Use this Bettership account?", check the email address and choose **Continue**. Your browser shows "Connected".

Claude only reads. It doesn't add, change, or remove anything in Bettership.

## Publish an item

1. In the desktop app, open **Apply → Studio** and choose the item you want Claude to use.
2. Stay on the tab the item opens on, named for its kind, such as **Skill**.
3. Under "Claude handoff prompt", beside **Copy prompt** and **Download files**, choose **Publish to AI tools**. A window lists the files to publish.
4. Choose **Publish**. The button changes to **Published to AI tools**.

If **Publish to AI tools** isn't there, Claude isn't connected yet. Follow the steps under Connect Claude first.

## Use it in Claude

Start a new chat and ask for what you need. You might ask:

- "What does my Bettership Library say about pricing?"
- "Use my interview prep skill from Bettership on this job description."

Claude shows "Used Bettership" when it reads from your account, and names the resources it drew on.

## Check it worked

In the desktop app, **Settings → Connectors & MCP** lists the connection under "Authorized connections" as "Connected", with the time Claude last read from it.

To stop Claude reading, choose **Disconnect** there or on the Bettership page in Claude's **Connectors** tab. Copies Claude already received stay in Claude.
