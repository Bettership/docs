When you're done, Claude can read your Library and use what Maker makes for it, by name in any chat.

## Before you start

- A Plus or Max plan. Beta access counts.
- The Claude desktop app.

## Connect Claude

1. In the desktop app, open **Settings → Connectors & MCP** and copy the address it shows. It's `https://api.bettership.ai/mcp`.
2. In the Claude desktop app, choose **Customize** in Claude's sidebar, then the **Connectors** tab.
3. Choose **Add** at the top right.
4. Choose **Add custom connector**.
5. Enter Bettership in **Name**.
6. Paste the address you copied in **MCP server URL**.
7. Choose **Continue**. Claude has already selected **Sign in now** and **Use Claude's published identity**. Leave them.
8. Choose **Add**. Claude opens its Bettership page, which says "You're not connected to Bettership yet."
9. Choose **Connect**. Your browser opens. If it asks "Finish connecting a connector?", choose **Continue connecting**.
10. On the Bettership page, choose **Allow Access**.
11. Sign in with **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or **Email me a code**.
12. On "Use this Bettership account?", check the email address and choose **Continue**. Your browser shows "Connected".

## Make an item for Claude

1. In the desktop app, open **Apply → Studio**.
2. Choose **Make new** at the top right. The "Make something new" window opens.
3. Under "How should it work?", choose **Through my Bettership connection** rather than **A self-contained download**.
4. Under "What should it do?", enter what it's for.
5. Choose **Make it**. Maker's chat opens and Maker starts.
6. Maker shows a status line such as "Sizing this up…" while it works. Wait for the item's card in its reply, with "Live" on it. Claude can now use it.

## Use it in Claude

In a new Claude chat, ask for what you need. For example:

- "What does my Bettership Library say about pricing?"
- "Use my interview prep skill from Bettership on this job description."

Claude shows "Used Bettership" when it reads your account, and names the resources it drew on.

## Pause or remove an item

Open **Apply → Studio**, choose the item's card, then choose **…** at the top right. In the menu, choose **Available in your AI tools**. In the window that opens:

- **Pause automatic updates** keeps later versions out of Claude.
- **Unpublish** takes the item out of your AI tools.

The desktop app also copies a published subagent into Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex when they're connected and on this computer. **Unpublish** takes those copies out too, except a file you've edited.

## Check it worked

In the desktop app, **Settings → Connectors & MCP** lists the connection under "Authorized connections" as "Connected". To stop Claude reading, choose **Disconnect** there. Claude keeps what it already received.
