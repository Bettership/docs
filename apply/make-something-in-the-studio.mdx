When you're done, you'll have a finished item in the Studio, ready for the AI tools you work in.

## Ask Maker for it

1. Open **Apply → Studio**.
2. Choose **Make new** at the top right. The "Make something new" window opens.
3. Under "What do you want to make?", choose **Let Maker choose**, **Prompt**, **Context pack**, **Skill**, or **Subagent**. If you're not sure, choose **Let Maker choose**. [Prompts, context packs, and skills](/apply/prompts-context-packs-and-skills) explains each.
4. Under "What should it do?", enter what you want it for. For example, "Help me get ready for a customer interview."
5. Under "What should it draw from?", any Focus Areas listed are included, and you can leave it at that. To add a resource, choose the box that reads "Search your resources, collections, and focus areas". A list opens.
6. Select a resource from the list.
7. Choose **Make it**. Maker's page opens with your request already sent, and Maker starts.

## Wait for Maker

While it works, Maker shows a short status line, such as "Sizing this up…". Then it shows a card for the item. When the card reads "Live", the item is finished.

## Take it to your AI tools

1. Open **Apply → Studio**.
2. Choose the card with your item's name. The item's page opens.
3. Under the item's name, find the button that starts **Add to**, such as **Add to Cursor**. It names the AI tool the item was made for.
4. To use a different tool, choose the small button joined to the right of it, the one with an arrow, then select a tool from the list. If yours isn't there, choose **Another tool…** at the bottom of that list, and select yours in the list that opens.
5. Choose the button that starts **Add to**. The files save as a .zip, a message for your AI tool goes on your clipboard, and the page reads "Files saved and message copied. Paste it into Cursor."

Then paste the message into a chat in that tool. It tells the tool how to set the item up from the .zip.

Some items have a different button there:

- **Copy the prompt**, for a prompt in a chat tool such as ChatGPT. Paste it at the top of a new chat.
- **Copy the role**, for a subagent in Claude Duet, ChatGPT Dots, or Grok Bot. Paste it into that agent's instructions.
- **Connect** and your tool's name, for an item that works through a connection to Bettership. [Use what you made in your AI tools](/apply/use-what-you-made-in-your-ai-tools) covers connecting.

## Check it worked

Its card in **Apply → Studio** reads "Live". On its page, "How to use it" has things to ask your AI tool.
