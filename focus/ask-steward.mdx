Steward helps you put your learning direction into words, keep what Bettership knows about you accurate, and find your way around the app.

## What to ask

These are example starting points:

- “Help me define a Focus Area around handling difficult feedback.”
- “What do you remember about my role? It has changed.”
- “Where can I change how often I get practice emails?”
- “What's the difference between my Library and Inbox?”

Steward can help define and manage Focus Areas, update supported profile details, and find, correct, or remove saved memories. When a change needs your confirmation, you'll see a card with the details before it is saved.

## Where to find Steward

Open **Settings** and choose **Ask Steward**. The **Agent chat** button in Settings also opens Steward beside the page you're viewing.

For context you've saved about yourself, **Manage memories** is available from **Profile** in Settings.

## When another agent can help

Scout handles resource assessment and collection changes. Coach owns practice. Maker creates reusable items. Steward can offer a handoff that carries the useful context into the next conversation. You choose whether to continue.

## What Steward cannot do

Steward cannot submit a support ticket or feedback on your behalf. Describing a problem in chat doesn't send it to a person.

Steward also cannot access payment details, operate a website you're signed in to, grant macOS permissions, or connect a local folder for you. Account deletion and sign-in changes remain your actions in Settings.
