Steward helps you set your Focus Areas, the things you want to get better at, keep what Bettership knows about you accurate, and find your way around the app.

## What to ask

These are example starting points:

- “Help me define a Focus Area around handling difficult feedback.”
- “What do you remember about my role? It has changed.”
- “Where can I change how often I get practice emails?”
- “What's the difference between my Library and Inbox?”

## Where to find Steward

Open **Settings** with the gear icon at the bottom of the left sidebar, to the right of your name and initials. Choose the button labelled **Agent chat** at the top right of any Settings page. A Steward panel opens beside the page you're viewing.

## What Steward does

Steward can help define and manage Focus Areas, update supported profile details, and find, correct, or remove saved memories. When a change needs your confirmation, you'll see a card with the details before it is saved.

Scout handles resource assessment and collection changes. Coach owns practice. Maker creates reusable items. When another agent is a better fit, Steward can offer a handoff that carries the useful context into the next conversation. You choose whether to continue.

Describing a problem to Steward doesn't send it to a person. When something in the app isn't working and you want a person to know, choose **Email support** in **Settings → Support**. Your mail app opens a new email to support@bettership.ai.
