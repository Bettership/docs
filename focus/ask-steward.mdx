Steward helps you set your Focus Areas (the things you want to get better at), keep what Bettership knows about you accurate, and find your way around the app.

## What to ask

These are example starting points:

- “Help me define a Focus Area around handling difficult feedback.”
- “What do you remember about my role? It has changed.”
- “Where can I change how often I get practice emails?”
- “Reword my Focus Areas now that my app has launched.”

## Where to find Steward

Your name is at the bottom of the sidebar on the left of the app. Choose the gear icon to the right of it. **Settings** opens. At the top right of the page, choose the **Agent chat** button. A Steward panel opens beside the page. Enter your question in the message box at the bottom of the panel.

## What Steward does

Steward can help define and manage Focus Areas, update your profile details, such as your role, and find, correct, or remove what it has saved about you. When a change needs your confirmation, you'll see a card with the details before it is saved.

When you ask Steward to reword or update saved Focus Areas, it drafts the new wording in the same chat. You get one card for each Focus Area it would change, showing the new wording above what's saved now, and Steward tells you which ones it left alone. Choose **Save these changes** to keep a draft, or **Skip** to leave that Focus Area as it is. The card then says which you chose. To change a draft, tell Steward what you'd like different, and a new card replaces it. A new Focus Area or a real change of direction gets a longer conversation with Steward first.

Scout handles resource assessment and collection changes. Coach owns practice. Maker creates reusable items. When another agent is a better fit, Steward can offer a handoff that carries the useful context into the next conversation. You choose whether to continue.

Describing a problem to Steward doesn't send it to a person. When something in the app isn't working and you want a person to know, choose **Email support** under **Support** in the Settings column on the left. Your mail app opens a new email to support@bettership.ai.
