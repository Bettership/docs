The same idea can turn up in a book, a podcast, and your workshop notes. The Wiki gives those connections a place to gather.

## Look across resources

Your Library starts with the things you bring in. The Wiki brings together the people, organizations, concepts, frameworks, and techniques found across them.

A resource answers “What did this article say?” A Wiki page helps you explore “Where else have I encountered this idea?” or “What do I have from this person?”

This makes it easier to return to an idea without remembering which resource introduced it.

## Use the connections, then check the material

Connections can help you compare explanations, notice a recurring method, or find another perspective. They don't make every interpretation correct. Return to the linked resource when you want the original context or need to check a claim.

Scout can help you investigate what's in your Library. Coach can help you work through what an idea means and whether you can use it.

## You don't have to build it by hand

The Wiki grows from the material Bettership processes. You can use it as a way back into your learning rather than as another filing system to maintain.
