Send feedback, a bug report, or a feature request from Settings, and follow where it stands.

## Send it from Settings

1. At the bottom of the left sidebar, choose the gear icon beside your name. Settings opens.
2. In the list on the left, under the small heading Support, choose **Feedback**. The Feedback page opens with a form at the top.
3. At the top of the form, choose one of three buttons: **Feedback**, **Bug report**, or **Feature request**. None is chosen when the form opens. The label on the big box changes to match: "What's on your mind", "What happened", or "What you'd like".
4. Enter what you want to say in that box. **Title (optional)** above it can stay empty.
5. For a bug report, you can choose **Choose image** to attach one screenshot. Its file name shows in place of the button, with **Remove** beside it.
6. Choose **It's okay to follow up with me about this** if you're happy to get an email about it. Nothing is sent to you otherwise.
7. Choose **Send**. It stays greyed out until you've picked a type and written something.

A bug report also carries your app version, the page you were on, and your macOS version, as the form says.

## Or ask Steward

On the Feedback page, choose the **Agent chat** button at the top right of the Settings window. A chat with Steward opens beside the page. Then ask Steward to send it for you. For example:

- "Please send a feature request: I'd like to sort my Library by the date I last opened a resource."
- "What happened to the things I've sent?"

Steward reads back what it will send and sends it only after you answer yes in the chat. What Steward sends shows in **What you've sent** too. Steward doesn't attach screenshots, so use the form when a picture would help.

## Check it worked

The form is replaced by "Thank you. We read every one. If it's urgent, Bettership hears about it right away." and then comes back empty. What you sent is at the top of **What you've sent**, below the form, with its type, its title, the date, and the status Received. Choose a row to read what you wrote.

## See where it stands

Each row's status changes as we act on it:

- **Planned** once it's scheduled
- **Looking into it** once work has started
- **Fixed** for a bug report, or **Built** for a feature request, when it's done
- **Not planned** if we decided against it

The page shows the latest status each time you open it: choose another page in Settings, such as **Profile**, then **Feedback** again.
