Send feedback, a bug report, or a feature request from Settings, then find it in the list of what you've sent.

## Send it from Settings

1. Open **Settings** from the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Choose **Feedback** under Support in the list on the left. The Feedback page opens with a form at the top.
3. Choose **Feedback**, **Bug report**, or **Feature request** at the top of the form. The label on the big box changes to match: "What's on your mind", "What happened", or "What you'd like".
4. Enter what you want to say in that box. **Title (optional)** above it is yours to fill or leave empty.
5. For a bug report, you can choose **Choose image** to attach one screenshot. Its file name shows in place of the button, with **Remove** beside it.
6. Choose **It's okay to follow up with me about this** if you're happy to get an email about it. Nothing is sent to you otherwise.
7. Choose **Send**. It stays greyed out until you've picked a type and written something.

A bug report also carries your app version, the page you were on, and your macOS version. The form says so under the box when you choose **Bug report**.

## Or ask Steward

On the Feedback page, choose the button labelled **Agent chat** at the top right. Steward opens beside the page. Then ask Steward to send it for you. For example:

- "Please send a feature request: I'd like to sort my Library by the date I last opened a resource."
- "What happened to the things I've sent?"

Steward reads back what it will send and sends it only after you answer yes in the chat. What Steward sends shows in **What you've sent** like anything you send from the form. Steward doesn't attach screenshots, so use the form when a picture would help.

## Check it worked

The form is replaced by "Thank you. We read every one." and then comes back empty. What you sent is at the top of **What you've sent**, below the form, with its type, its title, the date, and the status Received. Choose a row to read what you wrote.
