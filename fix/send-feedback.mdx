Send feedback, a bug report, or a feature request from Settings, and follow where it stands.

## Send it from Settings

1. In the bottom left corner, choose the gear icon to the right of your name. Settings opens.
2. In the list on the left, under the small heading Support, choose **Feedback**. The Feedback page opens with a form at the top.
3. At the top of the form, choose one of three buttons: **Feedback**, **Bug report**, or **Feature request**. None is chosen when the form opens. The label on the big box changes to match: "What's on your mind", "What happened", or "What you'd like".
4. Enter what you want to say in that box. **Title (optional)** above it can stay empty.
5. For a bug report, you can choose **Choose image** to attach one screenshot. Its file name shows in place of the button, with **Remove** beside it.
6. Choose **It's okay to follow up with me about this** if you're happy to get an email about it.
7. Choose **Send**. It stays greyed out until you've picked a type and written something.

A bug report also carries your app version, the page you were on, and your macOS version.

## Or ask Steward

Open the Feedback page (steps 1 and 2), then choose **Agent chat** at the top right. A chat with Steward opens beside the page. Then ask Steward to send it for you. For example:

- "Please send a feature request: I'd like to sort my Library by the date I last opened a resource."
- "What happened to the things I've sent?"

Steward reads back what it will send and sends it only after you answer yes in the chat. What Steward sends shows in **What you've sent** the next time you open the page. Steward doesn't attach screenshots, so use the form when a picture would help.

## Check it worked

The form is replaced by "Thank you. We read every one." and then comes back empty. What you sent is at the top of **What you've sent**, below the form, with its type, its title, the date, and the status Received. Choose a row to read what you wrote.

## See where it stands

Something sent as **Feedback** goes from Received to **Read** once our weekly review has read it.

Bug reports and feature requests change as we act on them:

- **Planned** once it's scheduled
- **Looking into it** once work has started
- **Fixed** for a bug report, or **Built** for a feature request, when it's done
- **Not planned** if we decided against it

Statuses don't change while the page is open. To see the latest, choose another page in Settings, such as **Profile**, then **Feedback** again.
