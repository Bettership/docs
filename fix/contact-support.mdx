Start with the question or the thing that didn't work. You don't need to know which part of Bettership is responsible.

## Ask Steward in the app

1. Open **Settings** with the gear icon at the bottom of the left sidebar, to the right of your name and initials.
2. Choose the button labelled **Agent chat** at the top right of the Settings page. A Steward panel opens beside the page you're viewing.

Steward can explain how Bettership works and help you find a setting. Describing a problem to Steward doesn't send it to a person.

## Email support

1. Choose your name and initials at the bottom of the left sidebar. A menu opens. The gear icon to their right opens Settings instead.
2. Choose **Email support**. Your mail app opens a new email to support@bettership.ai.

The email starts with two questions to answer before you send it: "What were you trying to do?" and "What happened instead?"

**Email support** is also in **Settings → Support**.

The email already lists your account, app version, the page you were on, and your system. You can delete any of them before you send.

If you can't open the desktop app, or no mail app opens, email [support@bettership.ai](mailto:support@bettership.ai) from any email account. Say what you were trying to do and what happened, including the exact error message.

Share only what's needed to explain the problem. You don't need to send a whole recording or private document to report an error.

## Questions about personal data

Use [privacy@bettership.ai](mailto:privacy@bettership.ai) for a copy of your personal data, a correction request, or deletion help if you can't access your account.
