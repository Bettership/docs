The app won't download or open, a permission says "Not allowed," or an update didn't go through.

## What you'll see

- Nothing downloaded, or the desktop app won't open.
- In **Settings → App preferences**, **Microphone** or **Screen Recording** says "Not allowed."
- Under the **Bettership** row in **Settings → App preferences**, a message starting "Couldn't reach the update server" or "The update couldn't be verified."
- A screen titled "Update to keep going."

## Why it happens

The desktop app needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 or later. Capture needs macOS permission for the microphone and Screen Recording. Updates need Bettership's update server, and an old version stops once a newer one is required.

## What to do

If the app won't download or open, check **General → About** in macOS System Settings. With an Apple M1 or newer **Chip** and **macOS** 13 or later, download again from [bettership.ai/download](https://www.bettership.ai/download) and follow [Install and sign in](/get-started/install-and-sign-in). On any other Mac, join the waitlist on that page.

If a capture permission says "Not allowed," fix one row at a time:

1. Choose **Settings**, the gear beside your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **App preferences**.
3. Under **System permissions**, choose **Open System Settings** beside the row that says "Not allowed." macOS opens that permission's list. Bettership is allowed when its switch is on.
4. Switch back to Bettership. The row says "Allowed" once macOS allows it.
5. If the row still says "Not allowed" with the switch on, choose **Bettership → Quit Bettership** in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
6. Open Bettership again from your Applications folder.

To check for an update:

1. In **Settings → App preferences**, choose **Check for updates** in the **Bettership** row. The button reads "Checking for updates…"
2. If the row says "You're up to date," you're done. If the button reads "Downloading update…," wait for **Restart to update**, then choose it.

If the server couldn't be reached, check your connection and try again. If the update couldn't be verified, install the latest version from [bettership.ai/download](https://www.bettership.ai/download) the same way as the first time.

The full-window **Update to keep going** screen shows one of these:

- **Restart to update**, once the new version downloads. Before that, a progress bar shows "Downloading version…"
- **Download the latest version**, when the app can't update itself, for example while you're signed out. It opens bettership.ai/download. Install it over this copy, then open Bettership again.

While the progress bar shows, **Download it from bettership.ai** under it opens the same download page.

## If that didn't work

Email [support@bettership.ai](mailto:support@bettership.ai) with the exact message and the version number in the **Bettership** row. If it won't open, include your chip and macOS version.
