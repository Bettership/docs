A resource you added shows a warning triangle on its tabs instead of its summary or full text.

## What you'll see

Open the resource. **Summary** and **Scout's report** read "This part couldn't be prepared because an earlier step failed." The full-text tab says what went wrong first. It is called **Article** on a web page and **Text** on a PDF, and a podcast or video calls it Transcript. For example:

- "The web extractor had a problem. Please try again." with a **Retry** button.
- "This article was too short to process. It may be blocked or incomplete. Try pasting the text or uploading a PDF."
- "We couldn't find any readable text in this PDF."

## Why it happens

Bettership couldn't get the text from the link or file. The summary and Scout's report are built from that text, so they wait for it. A message that says the page was too short, or that the PDF has no readable text, has no **Retry** button, because trying again would hit the same problem.

## What to do

1. In the sidebar, choose **Learn**.
2. Choose **Library**.
3. Choose the resource that didn't process.
4. Choose the full-text tab beside **Summary**: **Article** on a web page, **Text** on a PDF, or Transcript on a podcast or video.
5. If the tab has a **Retry** button, choose it. The button reads "Retrying…" while Bettership tries again. If the problem is still there, the message comes back.
6. If a web page or file has no **Retry** button, add the material another way. At the top of the window, choose **Add resource**.
7. In the dialog, choose the **Upload** tab.
8. Where it reads "Drop a file here, or choose one," drop the file there, or choose it. The dialog shows the file's name.
9. In the dialog, choose **Add resource**. The dialog closes, a "Resource added" notice appears, and the new resource shows in your Library.

## Or ask Scout

With the resource open, choose **Agent chat** at the top of the window. It opens a chat with Scout. Scout retries only the part you name: the content, the summary, or Scout's report. For example: "Retry the content for this resource." Scout says which part restarted.

## If that didn't work

A podcast or video with no **Retry** button has no other fix on this page. For any resource, contact support with the exact message and the link or file name.
