Coach helps turn an idea you recognize into something you can explain, try, and adapt.

## What to ask

For example:

- “Help me understand the argument in this article.”
- “Give me a short rep using this feedback framework.”
- “Help me prepare for a conversation where I need to disagree clearly.”
- “I tried this approach yesterday. Help me work through what happened.”

Bring a resource or describe the situation you want to work on. Coach can draw on your Library, notes, Focus Areas, and saved practice context. If the material doesn't answer something, Coach should distinguish its own explanation from what your resource says.

## Practice you can choose

Coach can prepare a one-off **rep** or help shape a **playbook** for recurring practice. A playbook can draw from a resource, collection, or Focus Area. You don't need a saved Focus Area to begin.

When Coach prepares a rep in conversation, the **Start the rep** card opens it. Coach gives feedback on the attempt and something specific to work on next.

You can ask Coach to adjust your practice or pause a playbook. You can also ask for a study guide or cheat sheet drawn from selected Library resources.

## Where to find Coach

Open **Practice → Coach**, or use **Agent chat** while you're in Practice.

## What Coach cannot do

Coach cannot observe a workplace attempt you haven't described or shared. It cannot treat reading, agreement, or a finished conversation as evidence that you've mastered a skill.

Coach doesn't create reusable prompts and skills for your other AI tools. That is Maker's job. Changes to your Focus Area definition go through Steward.

You can debrief when it's useful. Coach doesn't need a report after every real-world attempt before you can move on.
