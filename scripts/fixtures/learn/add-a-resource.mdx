You'll have a new resource at the top of your Library.

## Add a link

1. In **Learn**, choose **Add resource**.
2. Enter the link and choose **Add resource**. The dialog closes and the resource appears in your Library.

<img src="/images/learn/add-resource.png" style={{width: "100%"}} alt="The Add resource dialog with Link, File, Note, and Book tabs." />

## Or ask Scout

You might ask Scout to add a link you paste into the chat. Scout confirms what it added.

## Check it worked

The resource shows in the Library with its title. See [Focus Areas](/learn/focus-areas#one-goal-at-a-time) for what to do with it.
