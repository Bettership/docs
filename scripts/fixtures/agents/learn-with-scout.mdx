Scout looks after what comes into your Library and helps you find more of what fits.

## What to ask

For example:

- "Find me two podcasts about giving feedback."
- "Put these three resources in my Interview practice collection."
- "What's new in my Inbox this week?"

## Where to find Scout

Scout lives in **Learn**, on the **Learn with Scout** page.

## What Scout does

Scout reads what arrives in your Inbox and says how each piece fits your Focus Areas.
