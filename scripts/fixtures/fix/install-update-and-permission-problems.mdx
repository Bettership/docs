The desktop app won't open, won't update, or can't hear a capture.

## What you'll see

macOS shows "Bettership would like to record this computer's screen and audio."

## Why it happens

macOS asks once before any app can record.

## What to do

1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording**.
2. Turn on **Bettership**.
