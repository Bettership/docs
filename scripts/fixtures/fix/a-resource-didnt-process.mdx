The resource sits in your Library with a message where its summary should be.

## What you'll see

The resource shows "We couldn't process this resource."

## Why it happens

The page behind the link was private or empty when Bettership read it.

## What to do

1. Open the resource.
2. Choose **Try again**.

## If that didn't work

Write to [support@bettership.ai](mailto:support@bettership.ai) with the link you added.
