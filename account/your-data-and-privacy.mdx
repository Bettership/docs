Bettership keeps the material and context you bring so you can return to your learning and the agents can help from the same workspace.

## What's kept

This includes account and profile details, resources and extracted text, your notes, conversations, practice history, and things you've made. Bettership also keeps summaries and connections drawn from that material.

A capture can include its transcript, notes, screenshots, and images retained through **Watch window**. Transcripts are stored as spoken. Names, email addresses, and other personal details aren't automatically masked.

Outside services process your material to provide these features. Bettership also keeps diagnostic information to investigate errors and improve the product.

Session replay records how you use the desktop app, to show where something went wrong. What you type into fields and your own material are masked, and replays are kept for 30 days.

## What Scout sends to a search engine

When Scout looks for material on the web, it sends a search service a few words. They come from your message, the search topics on your Focus Areas, and a short list of public words, such as a format, a level, a platform name, or a recent year. Your notes, Library, and profile aren't sent.

Bettership removes your name, your employer, people in your workspace, links, email addresses, and numbers from search topics. You can read and edit them in **Settings → Focus areas**, under **Topics Scout can search for**. To stop Scout searching with them, turn off **Let Scout search the public web using your Focus Area topics** in **Settings → Data & privacy**. Scout then searches only with the words you type.

## Recordings and transcripts are different

Audio recordings normally stay on your computer for seven days to help recover transcription. The app clears old ones when it starts, so files can remain longer if Bettership isn't opened.

Removing local audio doesn't remove the saved transcript, which stays in your account until you remove it or request account deletion.

## Review or correct your personal context

Open **Settings → Profile → Manage memories** to review saved memories. You can also ask Steward what it remembers and request a correction or removal.

In **Settings → Data & privacy**, you can turn off **Let your Bettership agents keep memories about you**. The agents then stop adding what they infer or look up. What you tell them directly is still saved.

A profile field, such as your name or role, is separate from a saved memory. Check the Profile page when you want that field changed too.

## Take a copy or delete your account

A self-service account export isn't available yet. Email [privacy@bettership.ai](mailto:privacy@bettership.ai) to request a copy or help correcting personal data.

To delete your account, choose **Delete account** in **Settings → Sign-in & security**. Deletion is permanent, so request any copy first. Bettership aims to complete the request within 30 days or one calendar month, whichever comes first. Some diagnostic logs, backups, and records kept for legal reasons have separate retention periods. Capture files on another computer need to be removed there as well.
