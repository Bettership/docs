You'll send a friend your invite code. When they subscribe and make their first payment, you each get 25 credits.

## Share your code

1. Choose your name at the bottom of the left sidebar. The profile menu opens.
2. Choose **Invite a friend**. **Settings → Referrals & rewards** opens, with your code under **Your invite code**.
3. Choose how to send it:
   - **Copy** puts only the code on your clipboard. The button reads "Copied."
   - **Copy invite** puts a short message with your code and the Bettership website on your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere. The button reads "Invite copied."
   - **Email an invite** opens a new email in your mail app with that message filled in and no address yet.

## What your friend does

Your friend enters your code in **Settings → Billing & plans**, in the **Have a code?** field, and chooses **Redeem**. They need to do this before their first payment. Entering the code gives nothing on its own. You both get your credits when they pay for the first time.

Referral credits don't expire while you subscribe.

## Check it worked

On **Settings → Referrals & rewards**, **What you've earned** shows what your code has earned. Before anyone has entered your code, it reads "No one has used your code yet."
