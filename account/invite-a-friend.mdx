You'll send a friend your invite code. When they subscribe to any paid plan and make their first payment, you each get 25 credits.

## Share your code

1. Choose your name and email at the bottom of the left sidebar. The profile menu opens.
2. Choose **Invite a friend**. **Settings → Referrals & rewards** opens, with your code under **Your invite code**.
3. Choose how to send it:
   - **Copy** puts only the code on your clipboard. The button reads "Copied."
   - **Copy invite** puts a short message with your code and the Bettership website on your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere. The button reads "Invite copied."
   - **Email an invite** opens a new email in your mail app with that message filled in and no address yet.

## What your friend does

Send your friend these steps. They follow them in their own Bettership app, signed in to their own account, before their first payment:

1. Choose their name and email at the bottom of the left sidebar. The profile menu opens.
2. Choose **Settings**. Settings opens on **Profile**.
3. Choose **Billing & plans** in the Settings sidebar.
4. In the **Redeem a code** card on that page, enter the code you sent.
5. Choose **Redeem**. A line under the field says whether the code worked.

Entering the code gives nothing on its own. You both get your credits when they pay for the first time, and you can see yours under **What you've earned**.

Referral credits don't expire while you subscribe.

## Check it worked

On **Settings → Referrals & rewards**, **What you've earned** shows what your code has earned. Before anyone has entered your code, it reads "No one has used your code yet." After that, it shows **Subscribed with your code** and **Credits earned**.
