Credits pay for bringing things into Bettership and for making things. Talking with the agents and practicing never use them.

Say you add a long podcast episode, record a class, and ask Coach for a cheat sheet. Each of those uses credits, because each is real work: a transcript to read, audio to transcribe, something new to make. Asking Coach about the class afterward, or running a rep on it, costs nothing. That line is on purpose, so you never weigh a question or another attempt against your balance.

## What uses credits

| What you do | Credits |
|---|---|
| Add a resource | Half a credit or more, by length, so a long book costs more than a short post |
| Live capture, recording a class or call as it happens | 3 an hour, counted as half a credit for each 10 minutes or part of them |
| Make something with Maker | 1 for a prompt, 2 for a context pack, 3 for a skill |
| Make study material with Coach | 1 for a cheat sheet, 2 for a study guide |
| Talk with any agent | Never |
| Practice | Never |

Study material a playbook prepares for its own practice costs nothing. Podcasts and videos count by the length of their transcript. A resource's exact cost is set once Bettership has processed it, and it shows in Usage.

A capture is charged once, when it's saved, and nothing counts down while it records. Its cost then shows on the capture's page and in Usage. A capture under a minute costs nothing. Starting one needs at least half a credit. With less, the capture doesn't start, and the app offers extra credits or a plan instead. A capture that runs out of credits partway still finishes in full, and the extra time costs nothing.

## What each plan includes

| Plan | Credits | Unused credits you can keep |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 15, once | None |
| Core | 25 a month | None |
| Plus | 75 a month | Up to 40 |
| Max | 200 a month | Up to 100 |

Kept credits are spent before the new month's. Starter is a one-time allowance to try Bettership. It also includes 15 agent conversations, counted apart from credits, and after those, talking with the agents needs a paid plan.

On a paid plan you can buy **extra credits**: 20 for $10. They don't expire while you're subscribed, and they're spent last.

## When you run out

Nothing is lost. A resource that needs more credits than you have keeps its place in your Library and reads "Waiting for credits," with what it needs. It finishes on its own once credits arrive, from extra credits, your monthly credits, or resuming a paused plan. There's no need to add it again or retry it.

Your Library stays open, and the agents and practice keep working, because they never used credits. To get more sooner, see where your balance lives below.

## Where to see your balance

Choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar. The menu that opens shows your balance beside **Usage**. Choosing **Usage** opens **Settings → Account → Usage**, which lists what each credit went to. Its **See plans** link opens your plan choices and extra credits.
