When you're done, the Bettership desktop app is installed, you're signed in, and you know where to check its capture permissions.

## Install the desktop app

Download the desktop app from [bettership.ai/download](https://www.bettership.ai/download). Then:

1. Open the Bettership .dmg file in your Downloads folder. A window called "Install Bettership" opens.
2. Drag the Bettership icon into your Applications folder.
3. Open the Bettership app from your Applications folder. It opens on "Welcome to Bettership."

## Sign in

The same steps sign you in or, with a new email, create your account. You can also choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** on the same screen.

1. Enter your email in **Email**.
2. Choose **Send code**. "Check your email" opens.
3. Enter the eight-digit code from the email in **Confirmation code**. You're signed in as soon as the last digit goes in.
4. If the email is new, "Set up your account" opens. Enter your name in **Your full name**.
5. Select **I agree to Bettership's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.**
6. Choose **Finish setting up**. A short welcome from the Bettership agents opens.
7. Choose **Skip** if you'd rather go straight to Home.

## Check capture permissions

Live capture needs macOS access to your microphone and to Screen Recording.

1. Choose **Settings**, the gear beside your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Settings opens.
2. Choose **App preferences**.
3. Find **System permissions**, near the bottom of the page. The **Microphone** and **Screen Recording** rows each say "Allowed" or "Not allowed."
4. Choose **Open System Settings** beside a row that says "Not allowed." macOS System Settings opens on that permission's list, where Bettership has its own switch.
5. Come back to Bettership. The row shows the new setting.

## Check it worked

Home opens with a greeting and **Getting started** in the sidebar.
