Aim for one useful result this week: a clearer explanation, a better attempt, or something you can reuse. You don't need a full Library to get there.

## Name what you're working toward

Pick something you want to get better at. “Give clearer feedback” is enough to start. Add why it matters now: perhaps you keep leaving a conversation unsure whether your point landed.

That direction can become a [Focus Area](/how-bettership-works/focus-areas). Steward can help you put it into words. You can also begin with a resource or a question and define the direction later.

## Bring one meaningful resource

Choose something you've already decided is worth your attention: a chapter, article, podcast episode, workshop, or set of notes.

Scout can help you see what's in it and how it connects to your interests. A short resource you want to use is enough. There's no benefit to filling the Library before you know what you want from it.

## Do something with an idea

Choose the move that helps now:

- Ask Coach to help you explain the idea in your own words.
- Ask Coach for a practice rep tied to a situation you describe.
- Ask Maker to turn a useful method into a prompt you can reuse in your AI tool.

These are options, not a checklist. Understanding may be the whole job for today.

## Notice what changed

Can you explain a distinction you couldn't explain before? Did a practice attempt expose the part that needs work? Did a prompt help you ask better questions?

Those are useful early results. Finishing a resource or filling a page isn't the only way to tell whether the effort helped.

If you try something outside Bettership, you can bring the result back to Coach. A debrief is available when it helps, never a requirement before you move on.
