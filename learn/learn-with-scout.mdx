Ask Scout to find, read, compare, and organize the resources you learn from, in your Library and on the web.

## What to ask

For example:

- “What do I already have on product discovery?”
- “Compare how these two articles approach customer interviews.”
- “Help me find a good course for my Focus Areas.”
- “Add those to my Pricing research collection.”

Name resources by title in your ask.

## Where to find Scout

Open **Learn → Scout** for a full conversation. In Learn, **Agent chat** at the top right of the desktop app opens Scout beside what you're viewing.

## What Scout does

**Searches and reads your Library.** Scout finds what you have, reads your resources and your notes and highlights on them, and compares them.

**Finds new material.** Ask for material toward something you're working on, and Scout checks your Library, then searches, with nothing for you to type or approve. It uses your words and the search topics on your Focus Areas, and checks public resources Bettership has already processed. Each find is a card with a reason it fits you. A card marked “Ready to read now” is already in Bettership. To stop Scout searching with your topics, turn off **Let Scout search the public web using your Focus Area topics** in **Settings → Data & privacy**.

Nothing is added until you say so. Choose **Add to Library** on a card, or ask Scout later to add cards and file them in a collection. Each add spends credits.

**Finds courses.** Ask for a course, or name Maven, Coursera, edX, or LinkedIn Learning. Scout reads each course's page before showing it, so the card shows only what that page says: who offers and teaches it, format, level, length, rating with its review count, and price. **Open on** and the platform, such as **Open on Coursera**, goes to the course. **Save course** makes a collection for the course, with a link to its page. Saving adds nothing to your Library and spends no credits.

**Keeps your search topics.** Scout can add, remove, or reset the search topics on a Focus Area when you ask. See [Focus Areas](/focus/focus-areas) for what search topics are.

**Organizes when you ask.** Scout can create and edit the collections you made yourself, file resources into them, and manage the podcasts, newsletters, and channels you follow.

**Helps with your Inbox.** Tell Scout which Inbox items to keep in your Library and which to dismiss. It moves only what you name.

**Writes a report on a resource.** On each resource's page, Scout's report says what the resource offers and how it relates to your Focus Areas. Ask Scout to take another look, or to retry a resource that failed to process.
