When you're done, you'll have a resource open on its summary, full text, original pages, or Scout's report, with an agent ready to work on it.

## Open the resource

1. In the sidebar, choose **Learn**.
2. Choose **Library**. Your most recently added resources are at the top.
3. Choose a resource in the list. Its page opens on the **Summary** tab, with the full-text tab and **Scout's report** beside it.

## Read it

1. Choose **Summary** for an overview and the main ideas.
2. For the full text, choose **Article** on a web page or a note, **Transcript** on a podcast or video, **Book description** on a book added by its title, or **Text** on a PDF.
3. Choose **Scout's report** to see how the resource fits each of your Focus Areas.

## Read a PDF's pages

A PDF has an **Original** tab next to **Text**. The desktop app counts a PDF as a slide deck when most of its pages are wider than tall. On a slide deck, **Original** comes before **Text**. On other PDFs, it comes after. Either way, the page opens on **Summary**.

1. Choose **Original** to see the file's pages as the author made them, one under another. These pages are for looking, so you can't select words on them.
2. Choose **Text** to select or copy words.

In an agent's reply, a cited passage ends with a number in brackets, such as [1]. When the citation is to a slide deck, choose that number. The deck opens on **Original** with the cited slide at the top.

To open the file in another app, open the **…** menu, the three-dot button at the top right of the resource's page, and choose **Open in another app**. Your Mac opens the file outside the desktop app.

## Bring in an agent

At the foot of the page, choose one of three buttons. Each opens that agent's chat beside the page, with the resource attached and a few suggested asks.

- **Learn more** opens Scout, for example to find more like it.
- **Practice this** opens Coach, for example to quiz you on it.
- **Apply this** opens Maker, for example to make a prompt from it.

## While it's still being prepared

You can open a resource you've added before it's ready. Until then, the **Summary** tab reads "Preparing your summary," and **Learn more**, **Practice this**, and **Apply this** are unavailable.

## Check it worked

The resource's title is at the top of its page, with its tabs below it. Once it's ready, the three buttons at the foot of the page are available.
