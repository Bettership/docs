When you're done, you'll have a resource or capture open, filed where you want it, with an agent ready to work on it.

## Open the resource

1. In the sidebar, choose **Learn**.
2. Choose **Library**. Your most recently added resources are at the top.
3. Choose a resource in the list. Its page opens on the **Summary** tab, with the full-text tab and **Scout's report** beside it.

A capture in your Library opens the same way.

## Read it

1. Choose **Summary** for an overview and the main ideas.
2. For the full text, choose **Article** on a web page or a note, **Transcript** on a podcast, video, or capture, **Book description** on a book added by its title, or **Text** on a PDF.
3. Choose **Scout's report** to see how the resource fits each of your Focus Areas.

## Read a PDF's pages

A PDF has an **Original** tab next to **Text**. On a slide deck, where most pages are wider than tall, **Original** comes first.

1. Choose **Original** to see the file's pages as the author made them. You can't select words on them.
2. Choose **Text** to select or copy words.

When an agent cites a slide deck, choose the number in brackets, such as [1]. The deck opens on **Original** with the cited slide at the top.

To open the file in another app, open the **…** menu at the top right of the page and choose **Open in another app**.

## File it

Under the title are the Focus Areas, collections, and topics it's filed under. Bettership adds topics and connects Focus Areas while it prepares a resource or a capture.

1. Choose **Topic** in the row under them. A list of your topics opens.
2. Choose a topic. It appears with the others.

**Collection** and **Focus area**, beside **Topic**, work the same way. A capture can have up to three topics.

## Bring in an agent

At the foot of the page, choose one of three buttons. Each opens that agent's chat beside the page, with the resource attached and a few suggested asks.

- **Learn more** opens Scout, for example to find more like it.
- **Practice this** opens Coach, for example to quiz you on it.
- **Apply this** opens Maker, for example to make a prompt from it.

## While it's still being prepared

You can open a resource you've added before it's ready. Until then, the **Summary** tab reads "Preparing your summary," and **Learn more**, **Practice this**, and **Apply this** are unavailable.

## Check it worked

The resource's title is at the top of its page, with its tabs below it. Once it's ready, the three buttons at the foot of the page are available.
