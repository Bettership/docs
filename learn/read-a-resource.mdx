When you're done, you'll have a resource or capture open on its summary, full text, or Scout's report, filed where you want it, with an agent ready to work on it.

## Open the resource

1. In the sidebar, choose **Learn**.
2. Choose **Library** in the Learn group. Your most recently added resources are at the top.
3. Choose a resource in the list. Its page opens on the **Summary** tab, beside **Article** and **Scout's report**.

A capture in your Library opens the same way.

## Read it

1. Choose **Summary** for an overview and the main ideas.
2. Choose **Article** for the full text. A podcast, video, or capture names this tab **Transcript**, and a book added by its title names it **Book description**.
3. Choose **Scout's report** to see how the resource fits each of your Focus Areas, whether your Library already covers it, and the techniques it teaches.

## File it

Under the title are the Focus Areas, collections, and topics it's filed under. Bettership adds topics and connects Focus Areas while it prepares a resource or a capture.

1. Choose **Topic** in the row under them. A list of your topics opens, with a search field.
2. Choose a topic. The list closes and the topic appears with the others.

**Collection** and **Focus area**, beside **Topic**, open the same kind of list for your collections and Focus Areas. A capture can have up to three topics, and **Topic** is dimmed once it has them.

## Bring in an agent

At the foot of the page, choose one of three buttons. Each opens that agent's chat beside the page, with the resource attached and a few suggested asks.

- **Learn more** opens Scout, for example to find more like it.
- **Practice this** opens Coach, for example to quiz you on it.
- **Apply this** opens Maker, for example to make a prompt from it.

## While it's still being prepared

You can open a resource you've added before it's ready. Until then, the **Summary** tab reads "Preparing your summary," and **Learn more**, **Practice this**, and **Apply this** are greyed out and unavailable.

## Check it worked

The resource's title is at the top of its page, with its tabs below it. Once it's ready, the three buttons at the foot of the page are available.
