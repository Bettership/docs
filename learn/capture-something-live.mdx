When you're done, the capture is saved to your Library with your notes and a summary of what was said.

## Before you start

The desktop app needs two macOS permissions. **Microphone** lets it hear you, and **Screen Recording** lets it hear your computer's audio. To check them, choose **Settings**, the gear at the bottom of the sidebar, then **App preferences**. Under **System permissions**, each row reads **Allowed** once it's on. If a row doesn't, choose **Open System Settings** beside it. macOS System Settings opens, where you turn the permission on.

Only capture what you're allowed to. If anyone else can be heard, tell them Bettership is keeping a transcript.

## Capture

1. In the header, choose **Live capture**. The capture starts right away. The capture panel opens on the right, and the header button shows a running clock.
2. Enter a title in the field that reads "Untitled" at the top of the panel.
3. Write your notes in the space below the title.
4. To add the capture to a collection, choose **Collection** under the title. A list of your collections opens.
5. To follow one window, choose **Watch window**, the second icon from the left at the bottom of the panel. **Watch a window** opens with your open windows to choose from.
6. To stop for a while, choose **Pause** at the bottom of the panel. The status line above it reads "Paused."
7. To carry on, choose **Resume**, where **Pause** was.

## Finish

1. If the capture is running, choose **Pause** at the bottom of the panel. **Resume** and **Finish capture** appear in its place.
2. Choose **Finish capture**. The panel reads "Preparing your capture" and "Keep Bettership open until this is done." When it's done, the panel shows the summary.

## Check it worked

The panel shows "Capture saved to your Library," the summary, and **Open capture**. If no speech was heard, the summary is made from your notes, and "No speech detected in the audio. Generated from your notes." appears above it.

To find the capture later, choose **Type to search…** at the top of the app and enter its title.
