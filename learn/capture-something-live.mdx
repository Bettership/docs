When you're done, the capture is saved to your Library with your notes and a summary of what was said.

## Before you start

The desktop app needs two macOS permissions. **Microphone** lets it hear you, and **Screen Recording** lets it hear your computer's audio. To check them, choose **Settings**, the gear at the bottom of the sidebar, then **App preferences** in the Settings sidebar. Under **System permissions**, each row reads **Allowed** once it's on. If a row doesn't, choose **Open System Settings** beside it and turn it on in macOS.

Only capture what you're allowed to. If anyone else can be heard, tell them Bettership is keeping a transcript.

## Capture

1. In the header at the top of the app, choose **Live capture**, beside **Add resource**. The capture panel opens on the right, and the header button shows a running clock.
2. Enter a title in the field that reads "Untitled" at the top of the panel.
3. Write your notes in the space below the title.
4. To add the capture to a collection, choose **Collection** under the title. A list of your collections opens.
5. To follow one window, choose **Watch window**, the eye-in-a-frame icon beside **Pause** at the bottom right of the panel. **Watch a window** opens, listing your open windows.
6. To stop for a while, choose **Pause**. The status at the bottom left reads "Paused."
7. To carry on, choose **Resume**, where **Pause** was.

## When a capture goes quiet

If almost nothing has been said for 10 minutes and you haven't used the panel, the panel asks "Still capturing?" A notification asks too. Choose:

- **Keep going** to carry on.
- **Pause** to pause.
- **Finish capture** to finish.

With no answer, it pauses itself five minutes later. The panel reads "Paused at" the time, "after 15 minutes of quiet."

A capture also pauses when your computer sleeps, and stays paused when it wakes. Locking your screen doesn't pause it. When you come back, the panel opens itself and reads "Paused when your computer went to sleep."

After an automatic pause, choose **Resume** or **Finish capture**. To throw it away instead, choose **Discard** under the "Paused" line, then confirm.

## Finish

1. If the capture is running, choose **Pause** at the bottom right of the panel. It turns into two buttons, **Resume** and **Finish capture**.
2. Choose **Finish capture**. The panel reads "Preparing your capture" and "Keep Bettership open until this is done." When it's done, the panel shows the summary.

## Check it worked

A message reads "Capture saved to your Library" with **Open capture**.

To find the capture later, choose **Type to search…** at the top of the app and enter its title. It appears under "Captures."
