A collection keeps related resources together, such as a course or a reading list.

## Create a collection

1. In the sidebar, choose **Learn**.
2. Choose **Collections** in the row of tabs at the top (Library, Inbox, Collections, Wiki, Scout).
3. Choose **Add collection** at the top right of the list. A dialog opens on its **Collection** tab.
4. Enter a name.
5. Open **Additional details** to add a cover image or your own label, such as "Reading list."
6. Choose **Add collection** at the bottom of the dialog. The collection appears in your list.

## File a resource

1. In **Library**, choose the resource to open it.
2. Choose **+ Collection** beneath its title. A list of your collections opens.
3. Select the collection you want.
4. Choose **Add (1)** at the bottom of that list. The collection's name appears beneath the resource's title.
5. Choose that name to open the collection and see the resource in it.

## Archive a collection

Archiving takes a collection off your list without deleting anything. Its resources stay in your Library.

1. In **Collections**, choose **⋯** (three dots) at the right end of the collection's row.
2. Choose **Archive**. The row leaves the list and stays hidden until you show archived items under **Filters**.

Your list also shows publications, the shows and sites behind resources you added. Their rows say a kind like Publication or Article, where yours say Collection. A publication archives the same way, and stays off the list as more arrives from it.

## Find an archived collection

1. In **Collections**, choose **Filters**, beside **Add collection** at the top right.
2. Select the **Show only archived** checkbox, the last option in the panel. Only archived items show, and **Archived** appears beside the count at the top, such as "1 COLLECTION."
3. Choose **⋯** at the right end of the archived collection's row.
4. Choose **Unarchive**. The row leaves this view.
5. Choose the **×** right after **Archived**, beside the count. Your whole list is back, with the collection in it.

## Or ask Scout

In **Learn**, choose the **Scout** tab and enter your ask in the box at the bottom. For example:

> Put the article on interview questions in my Interview practice collection.

> Archive my Interview practice collection.

When you ask it to file resources, Scout replies with what changed, with nothing to confirm. Before it archives a collection, it shows a box in the chat with an **Archive collection** button, and nothing is archived until you choose it. Scout can also bring back an archived publication when you ask.

## Check it worked

Your collection is listed in **Collections**, and its name shows beneath each resource you filed in it.
