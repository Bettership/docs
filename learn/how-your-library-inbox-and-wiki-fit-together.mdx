Your Library holds the resources you've chosen to learn from. Your Inbox holds arrivals you haven't chosen to keep yet.

## Library: you've chosen it

An article you add, a note you write, or a capture you save belongs in your Library. Adding it is the decision to keep it. It doesn't take a detour through the Inbox because it is long or needs processing.

A Library resource can include its original content, a summary, and Scout's read on what is in it for you. You can bring it into a conversation, practice from it, or draw on it when making something.

Keeping a resource doesn't mean you've read or understood it. The Library preserves material you can work with.

## Inbox: decide whether to keep it

New items from publications you follow arrive in your Inbox. You can keep an item to bring it into the Library or dismiss it.

Scout can help you assess those arrivals and carry out the choices you ask for. Following a publication doesn't mean committing to read every item it publishes.

## A collection groups related resources

A collection might hold the material for a course, a book, or a subject you're exploring. It helps keep related resources together. A Focus Area answers a different question: what you want to get better at.

You can use both without treating them as interchangeable. A course collection holds the course material. Your Focus Area might be the communication skill you're learning from that course.
