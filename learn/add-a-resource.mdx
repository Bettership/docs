When you're done, the resource is in your Library, which you open from the sidebar under **Learn**.

## Open the dialog

1. On the right of the header at the top, choose **Add resource**. The **Add a resource to your library** dialog opens on the **Link** tab, beside **Upload**, **Note**, and **Book**. Each tab has its own **Add resource** button at the bottom right.

## Add a link

1. Paste the link into the field that reads **Paste a link to a web page, article, social post, podcast, or video**.
2. Choose **Add resource**. The dialog closes.

## Upload a file

1. Choose **Upload**. It takes PDF, DOCX, PPTX, MD, TXT, VTT, PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, and MOV.
2. Choose the area that reads **Drop a file here, or choose one**. A file picker opens.
3. Pick the file. Its name appears in that area.
4. Choose **Add resource**. The dialog closes.

## Write a note

1. Choose **Note**.
2. Enter a title in the **Title** field.
3. Enter the note's text in the field that reads **Paste or write anything you want to keep**. **Add resource** becomes available once both fields are filled.
4. Choose **Add resource**. The dialog closes.

## Add a book

1. Choose **Book**.
2. Enter the book's title in the field that reads **Title, ISBN, or link**. Matching books appear below it. If the tab says **No matches found**, try a different title.
3. Choose the book in the list. The tab asks **Add this book?** and shows its cover, title, authors, and year. Nothing is added yet. If it's the wrong book, choose **Back** at the bottom left to return to the list.
4. Choose **Add resource**. The dialog closes.

## Or ask Scout

1. In the sidebar on the left, choose **Learn**. **Scout** appears below it.
2. Choose **Scout**. Its chat page opens.
3. In the message field at the bottom of the page, enter a message with the link in it. For example: "Please add this to my Library:" followed by the link.
4. Press Return.

Scout replies in the chat. When it has added the link, it says so.

## Check it worked

After the dialog closes, a **Resource added** message appears in the lower right. Choose **Open resource** on it to go there. The message closes on its own. If you asked Scout, its reply confirms it.

Either way, the resource is then at the top of your Library. To get there, choose **Learn** in the sidebar, then **Library**. It's marked **Processing** until it's ready, and you can open it before then.
